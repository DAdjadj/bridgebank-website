Setup
15 minutes to set up. Then it runs forever.

Not one-click, but straightforward. You need Docker, 15 minutes, and a free Enable Banking account.

1
Create an Enable Banking account
Register at enablebanking.com and create an application. This is the regulated open banking provider that connects Bridge Bank to your bank.
⏱ ~5 min
2
Run the Docker container
Download the docker-compose.yml and run docker compose up -d. Works on Linux, Mac, Raspberry Pi, NAS.
⏱ ~2 min
3
Follow the setup wizard
Open Bridge Bank in your browser. The wizard walks you through entering your licence key, connecting to Actual Budget, and uploading your Enable Banking key.
⏱ ~5 min
4
Connect your bank
Search for your bank, approve read-only access, pick an Actual Budget account. Transactions sync automatically from here.
⏱ ~3 min
