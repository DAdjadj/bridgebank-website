How it works

Up and running in minutes

A few minutes of setup, then it runs on its own. No config files, no cron jobs, no scripts.

1
Create a free Enable Banking account
Register at enablebanking.com and create an application. This is the regulated open banking provider that gives Bridge Bank read-only access to your bank. Free, takes 5 minutes.
2
Install Bridge Bank
Download the docker-compose.yml file and run docker compose up -d on your server. Works on any machine with Docker — Linux, Mac, Raspberry Pi, NAS.
3
Follow the setup wizard
Open Bridge Bank in your browser. The setup wizard walks you through entering your licence key, connecting to Actual Budget, uploading your Enable Banking key, and configuring notifications.
4
Connect your bank
Search for your bank, approve read-only access, and choose a sync schedule. Bridge Bank handles everything from here — transactions sync automatically.
