How it works

Up and running in minutes

Everything runs through a browser-based wizard. No config files to edit, no terminal commands beyond docker compose up.

1
Get a licence key
One-time €29 payment. Your key arrives by email instantly. Or start a free 7-day trial.
2
Run the Docker container
One docker compose up -d command. That's it for the terminal.
3
Walk through the setup wizard
Enter your key, Actual Budget details, upload your Enable Banking key, and configure notifications — all in the browser.
4
Connect your bank
Search for your bank, approve read-only access, and Bridge Bank syncs transactions on your schedule.

What to do next

1
Place the downloaded docker-compose.yml in ~/bridge-bank/ on your server
2
Start the container: docker compose up -d
3
Open Bridge Bank in your browser and follow the setup wizard. You will upload your .pem file directly in the wizard — no terminal commands needed.
4
Confirm it's running: docker compose logs -f
⚠️
Bank sessions expire every ~180 days
Enable Banking requires you to re-authorise access roughly every 6 months. Re-run the bank authorisation command from step 3 above, then restart with docker compose restart. If you configured email alerts, you'll get a notification before expiry.
