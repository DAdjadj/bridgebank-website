FAQ

Questions

Can I use it to leave YNAB?

Yes, that's what the pair is for. Bridge Budget imports your YNAB budget straight from YNAB with a personal access token: accounts, transactions, categories, monthly budgets, targets and scheduled transactions. Bridge Bank then keeps your European bank flowing in automatically, the way YNAB's linked accounts did.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. €29 once, no subscription and nothing to renew. Updates are included.

Will it work with my setup?

If you self-host Actual Budget, you're most of the way there. Bridge Bank runs as a small Docker container right next to it. The bank connection goes through Enable Banking, which is free, and the setup guide walks you through it.

The 7-day trial is the easiest way to find out.

Is it safe to connect my bank?

Access is read-only and goes through Enable Banking, a regulated open banking provider. You approve it on your bank's own login page, so Bridge Bank never sees your bank password and can't make payments.

Bridge Bank runs on your machine and sends transactions straight to your Actual Budget. The code is open source.

Which banks work?

Over 2,500 banks across 29 European countries. Search for yours above.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. Syncing pauses until you add a license: buy one for €29, enter the key on the License page, and it starts again with your setup exactly as you left it.

Do I have to log in to my bank again?

Banks ask you to renew open banking access every 90 to 180 days. Bridge Bank emails you before it expires, and renewing takes one click and your bank login.

Can I connect more than one bank?

Yes. Two bank accounts are included, each syncing into its own Actual Budget account. Extra slots are €10 each from inside the app.

I don't have Docker. Can you host it?

Hosting on PikaPods is coming soon. Leave your email and you'll hear when it's ready.
