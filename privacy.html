Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Bridge Bank is a self-hosted application. Your bank transactions, credentials, and financial data never leave your machine — they are never sent to or stored on our servers. The only data we handle is what is strictly necessary to operate the licence system and, during bank connection, a short-lived encrypted authorisation code that we cannot read. This policy explains what that is.

Who we are

Bridge Bank is an independent software product developed and maintained by David Alves, Portugal. You can reach us at support@david-alves.com.

What data we collect

We collect only the minimum necessary to operate the licence system:

  • Your email address — collected by Stripe when you purchase a licence, and stored on our licence server to deliver your licence key and provide support.
  • Licence activation records — when you activate your licence key, we store a anonymised machine fingerprint (a hash of your hostname) and the number of active activations. We do not store device names, IP addresses, or any personally identifiable hardware information.
We do not collect, store, or have access to your bank account data, transaction history, Actual Budget content, Enable Banking credentials, SMTP passwords, or any financial information. All of this stays on your machine. During bank connection we briefly hold an encrypted authorisation code that we cannot read (see "Bank connection relay" below).

What we do not collect

  • Bank transactions or account balances
  • Actual Budget database content or passwords
  • Enable Banking OAuth tokens or credentials
  • SMTP passwords or email content
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • IP addresses or device identifiers

Bank connection relay

When you connect or re-authorise a bank, the bank confirms the authorisation with a short-lived authorisation code. To complete the connection reliably, this code can pass through Bridge Bank infrastructure. It is encrypted in your browser with a key held only by your own Bridge Bank installation, it is stored for at most 30 minutes and then deleted, and it cannot be read by us. It contains no account data, transactions, or balances, and it gives us no access to your bank.

Third-party services

Stripe — processes payments. When you purchase Bridge Bank, Stripe collects your email and payment information. Their privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

Enable Banking — provides open banking API access to your bank on your behalf. Their privacy policy governs how they handle your banking authorisation. Bridge Bank stores the resulting session tokens locally on your machine only.

Actual Budget — Bridge Bank writes transaction data to your self-hosted Actual Budget instance. You control that data entirely.

How we use your data

Your email address is used solely to:

  • Deliver your licence key after purchase
  • Respond to support requests you initiate
  • Send important notices about your licence

We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

We retain your email address and licence activation records for as long as your licence is active or until you request deletion. You can request deletion at any time by emailing support@david-alves.com. Stripe's retention policies apply to payment records.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email support@david-alves.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Bridge Bank after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, email support@david-alves.com. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
