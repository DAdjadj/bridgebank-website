Questions

How do I move from YNAB?
Open YNAB's Developer settings, click New Token, type your YNAB password and click Generate, then copy the token shown at the top (YNAB shows it only once). In Bridge Budget, choose Import file, then YNAB, and paste it. Your accounts, transactions, categories, monthly budgets, targets and scheduled transactions come across. The token is used once from your browser and never saved, and your YNAB account isn't changed. You can delete the token in YNAB afterwards.
Is this Actual Budget?
It is built on Actual's open-source engine, so your data, sync and bank imports work the same way. The budget screen, targets, Auto-Assign, schedule links, reports and assistant connector are Bridge Budget's own.
Where does my data live?
On your server or your computer, the same as Actual. Nothing is stored by us. If you connect an assistant, it talks to your own server through a private link, and nothing passes through us.
Do I need Bridge Bank?
No. You can import files or type transactions. Bridge Bank adds automatic sync from 2,500+ European banks, and works with Bridge Budget out of the box.
Does Bridge Budget use AI?
No. There is no AI inside Bridge Budget and it never calls an AI service. If you already use the Claude or ChatGPT app, you can connect it to your budget yourself, on the plan you already have, with no API key. Without one, everything works the same.
What happens when the trial ends?
Nothing is lost. Your budget opens on the classic Actual screen, which stays free, with a note at the top to buy or enter a key. A licence brings targets, Auto-Assign and the reports back exactly as you left them.
How do updates work?
Updates are included. Bridge Budget tells you when a new version is out, and one command installs it. Before a new version runs, it saves a copy of your budgets (the last five are kept), so you can always go back.
What can it run on?
Anything that runs Docker: a NAS, a home server, a Mac or Windows PC, or a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 with a 64-bit system. The setup guide has the one command.
Does it work outside Europe?
Yes. Bridge Budget works in any currency and any country. Only Bridge Bank's automatic bank sync is for European banks; anywhere else you can import files or connect another bank sync.
